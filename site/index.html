Frequently asked questions

What is a prompt?

A prompt is the question or instruction you give an AI tool. You can include your goal, background, and the type of answer you want.

Does ProshnoLab answer my question?

The builder prepares a prompt for you. Copy it into an AI tool, such as ChatGPT or Gemini, to get a response.

Can I use Bangla?

Yes. Use the language button to switch between Bangla and English.

Do I need an account or payment?

The ProshnoLab preview is free to use without an account. Your chosen AI tool may have its own sign-in or pricing.

Where do my answers go?

ProshnoLab processes your answers in the browser or on your phone. If you paste the prompt into another service, that service handles the text under its own privacy policy.
