Frequently asked questions

Can I browse in another language?

Use the site’s language button for English or Bangla. For more languages, open your browser’s Translate Page menu and choose a language.

How to translate a page in Chrome
What is a prompt?

A prompt is the question or instruction you give an AI tool. You can include your goal, background, and the type of answer you want.

Does Ask Better answer my question?

The builder prepares a prompt for you. Copy it into an AI tool, such as ChatGPT or Gemini, to get a response.

Can I use Bangla?

Yes. Use the language button to switch between Bangla and English.

Do I need an account or payment?

The Ask Better preview is free to use without an account. Your chosen AI tool may have its own sign-in or pricing.

Where do my answers go?

Ask Better processes your answers in the browser or on your phone. If you paste the prompt into another service, that service handles the text under its own privacy policy.

Compare the answers

Same task, two prompts. These sample answers illustrate how more context can reduce follow-up messages. Actual AI replies can vary.

Short prompt

Write an email asking for an extension.

Sample answer

Subject: Request for an extension Hello, Could I have more time to complete the assignment? Please let me know if an extension is possible. Thank you, [Your name]

Ask Better prompt

Write a polite email to my instructor asking to move my research outline deadline from Friday to Monday. Explain that I need the weekend to verify two sources. Include a subject line and keep it under 90 words.

Sample answer

Subject: Request to submit my research outline on Monday Dear Professor [Name], Could I submit my research outline on Monday instead of Friday? I’d like to use the weekend to verify two sources before turning it in. Thank you for considering my request. [Your name]

The second answer includes the assignment, the new deadline, and the reason. Try both prompts in the same AI tool to see what you get.
